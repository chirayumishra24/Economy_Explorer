# Teacher Guide: The Economy Machine
**Class 6 Social Science · Chapter 14: "Economic Activities Around Us"**

---

## 1. Lesson Objectives & Core Acceptance Criteria
By the end of this 25–35 minute session, every student should be able to:
1. **Define Roles**: Say what a *producer*, a *seller/trader*, and a *consumer* each do.
2. **Trace Value Chains**: Trace a product (e.g. cotton shirt or milk) from raw material to consumer in at least 3 stages.
3. **Classify Sectors**: Classify everyday activities into *Primary* (drawing from nature), *Secondary* (making/transforming), and *Tertiary* (supportive services) with a clear reason.
4. **Explain Interdependence**: Name what happens when one part of the economy is disrupted and explain who is affected.

## 3. Classroom Smartboard Best Practices

- **Enable Classroom Mode (👨‍🏫 button)**: Scales text and interactive buttons to 125% and removes individual score chips to prevent student distraction.
- **Audio Feedback**: Turn Sound ON (speaker icon) on smartboard speakers. The synthesized chimes provide audio confirmation of correct connections.
- **Two-Tap Connection**: On touchscreens or smartboards, students do not need to drag. Tap the starting node once, then tap the target node.
- **Teacher Jump Control**: Use the "Jump to..." dropdown in the top-left to skip directly to any stop during demonstrations or revision.

## 4. Key Concept Cheat Sheet (NCERT Whitelist)

- **Economic Activity**: Work people do to earn an income, livelihood, or produce value.
- **Primary Sector**: Activities drawing directly on nature (farming cotton, dairy cattle, timber forestry, fishing).
- **Secondary Sector**: Activities turning raw natural materials into finished goods (spinning thread, weaving cloth, pasteurizing milk, carpentry).
- **Tertiary Sector**: Supportive services that connect producers and consumers (truck transport, cooperative banking, bazaar shops, schools, healthcare).
- **Interdependence**: Sectors need each other. If transport stops, factories cannot weave and shop shelves stay empty.
