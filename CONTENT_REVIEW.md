## 3. Disruption Scenarios (What-If Ripple Effects)

1. **Highway Flooded (Truck Route Blocked)**:
   - *Consequences*: Transport stopped → Raw materials trapped at farms → Factories lack inputs → Bazaar shelves empty → Consumers cannot buy.
   - *Vocabulary check*: Pure physical flows (more/less/none), zero price/inflation mechanisms.
   - *Status*: `unverified`

2. **Severe Drought (Cotton Crop Fails)**:
   - *Consequences*: Farming harvest fails → No cargo for trucks → Looms sit idle at mill → Fewer shirts in shops.
   - *Status*: `unverified`

3. **Power Outage (Textile Mill Halts)**:
   - *Consequences*: Machinery stops → Raw cotton piles up in yard → Finished shirts stop arriving at shop.
   - *Status*: `unverified`

4. **Cooperative Bank System Maintenance**:
   - *Consequences*: Loan counters pause → Farmers and drivers face delays buying seeds and fuel.
   - *Status*: `unverified`
